Updating and redistribution
---------------------------
Retain LICENSE and these notices when redistributing the application or source.
Both texts are embedded in the executable and viewable through Settings > Licenses.
Release downloads also provide the texts separately; evela.zip contains only
the executable, including its embedded notices.

After dependency, asset or compiler changes, review the applicable upstream terms
and regenerate with: python tools/licenses/generate.py
The release workflow checks for stale module notices before building and refreshes
native-runtime notices from the compiler installation used for that release.
